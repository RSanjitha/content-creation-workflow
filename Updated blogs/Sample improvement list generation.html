Critical Issues — fix before publishing

Any reader who follows this recommendation will find a dead product. Replace with current tools: Optimizely, VWO, AB Tasty, or Google Ads built-in Experiments.
  • [FACTUAL ERROR — WRONG COMPANY NAME] Case Study 1 refers throughout to "Bannersnack". Bannersnack rebranded to Creatopy in February 2021 — over 4 years ago. The company no longer operates under this name. Update all references to "Creatopy (formerly Bannersnack)".

High-Priority Improvements

  • [AEO — MISSING DIRECT ANSWER BLOCK] The intro does not answer the core question ("what is A/B testing for display banner ads") in the first 40–60 words. A direct answer block is required for Google AI Overview and Perplexity eligibility. Add a bolded 2–3 sentence plain-language answer immediately after the H1.
  • [SEO — NO QUICK ANSWER TABLE] No summary table near the top. For a guide-format post this length, a Quick Answer table (what to test, minimum duration, top tools, sample size rule) immediately after the intro dramatically improves featured snippet eligibility.
  • [SEO/STRUCTURE — NO TABLE OF CONTENTS] The post exceeds 2,000 words with 8 H2 sections and 12+ H3s. A jump-to table of contents is required per CORE-EEAT O02.
  • [STAT — UNVERIFIED ROI CLAIM] The intro claims A/B testing can "boost ROI by up to 30%" — no source is cited. This is the first number a reader sees. Either attribute it ("According to [source]...") or replace with the cited case study result (Creatopy: 25% CTR increase). Floating unsourced stats reduce Trust score.
  • [VOICE — THIRD-PERSON SLIPPAGE] Multiple sections address "marketers" and "businesses" instead of "you/your": "Marketers are forced to make educated guesses", "Businesses may learn a lot about what works". Rewrite all third-person reader references to second-person throughout.
  • [CONTENT GAP — NO PREDIS SECTION] No "How to do this with Predis" section. Every how-to and guide post must include a brand-integrated workflow section. Predis's ad creative generator lets users produce multiple display ad variants in one session — this is a direct fit for the A/B testing workflow.
  • [SEO — TITLE WEAK] "A Guide to A/B Testing Your Display Banner Ads" starts with "A Guide to" — a weak, generic construction. Recommended updated title: "A/B Testing Display Banner Ads: A Complete Guide to Higher CTR" (52 chars, keyword-first, outcome in title).
  • [AEO — FAQ ANSWERS TOO BRIEF] All 5 FAQ answers are one or two sentences. FAQ schema requires substantive answers (3–5 sentences minimum) to be eligible for Google FAQ results and AI engine citations. Every FAQ answer needs to be expanded with actionable detail.

Rewrite in active voice with second-person address.
