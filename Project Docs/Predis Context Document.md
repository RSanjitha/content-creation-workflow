**2. What Predis.ai Does**

Predis.ai is an **AI-powered social media content and ad creation platform**. Users input text prompts, product URLs, or images and the platform generates ready-to-publish creatives --- static ads, video ads, carousels, reels, stories, UGC-style videos, and memes --- across every major social platform.

**Core product pillars**

**AI Ads**

- Static ad creatives (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Pinterest, TikTok, YouTube)

- Video ads (product ads, UGC-style ads without actors, animated ads)

- Ad copy, headlines, captions, hashtags --- all AI-generated

- A/B testing via multiple creative variations

**AI Creatives (organic social)**

- Social media posts (single image, carousel, stories)

- Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok videos

- AI meme generator, quote-to-posts, special day posts

- E-commerce product posts from catalog

**Publish & Schedule**

- Content calendar and scheduler

- Auto-post (up to 3 posts/day/brand on top plans)

- Multi-account publishing (up to 60 social channels)

- Team collaboration + approval workflows

**Competitor Intelligence**

- Social media competitor analysis

- Data-backed insights on what\'s working for competitors

**API**

- Text-to-video, text-to-post, meme, carousel, Instagram story, TikTok --- all available via API

- Targeted at developers and enterprises embedding Predis into their own products

**7. Brand Voice & Writing Style**

**Overall tone**

**Confident, punchy, results-first.** Predis speaks like a growth-focused marketer --- not a tech company. It leads with outcomes (CTR, ROAS, time saved) and uses direct second-person address. Sentences are short. Headlines are action-oriented.

**Homepage copy patterns**

- Commands and imperatives: *\"Create Winning Video Ads\"*, *\"Stop the scroll\"*, *\"Don\'t just test ads, start with the winning ones\"*

- Pain-first framing: *\"No ad campaign deck, no constant brainstorming, and no more publishing panic\"*

- Superlative efficiency claims: *\"10x cheaper\"*, *\"Save 90% of time & cost\"*, *\"in seconds\"*

- Social proof anchoring: numbers are specific (6.4M users, 200M ads, 75% reduction)

- Colloquial/punchy lines: *\"Get Envied by the Best Copywriters\"*, *\"No Guesswork\"*, *\"Sit back and relax\"*

- Emoji used sparingly in UX: checkmarks (✓), rocket (🚀), heart (♥) for social proof sections

**Blog writing style (predis.ai/resources/blog/)**

- Blog tagline: *\"Stories About How To Win On Social Media 🎯\"*

- Titles use listicles, numbered posts, and practical how-to framing: *\"11 Social Media Lead Generation Tactics\"*, *\"60+ Social Media Marketing Statistics\"*, *\"8 Best CapCut Alternatives\"*

- Topics: platform-specific guides (Instagram, TikTok, YouTube), social media strategy, tool comparisons, definitions/explainers

- Tone in blog: educational but approachable, avoids heavy jargon

- Predis blog is a **high-volume SEO content engine** --- 93+ pages of posts, covering broad social media topics (not just Predis features), designed to pull in top-of-funnel traffic

**What to avoid in Predis voice**

- Overly technical or academic language

- Passive voice

- Long qualifying sentences

- Generic AI buzzwords without outcome framing (e.g. don\'t say \"leverages cutting-edge AI\" --- say \"cuts ad creation time by 75%\")

**13. Content Quality Standards**

**Zero Plagiarism Policy**

All content generated by agents must be 100% original:

- **Never copy or closely paraphrase** competitor articles, external blog posts, or any third-party source --- even when used for research.

- **Ideas and facts can be drawn from research**, but every sentence must be rewritten entirely in Predis\'s own voice before output. Synonymizing existing sentences is not sufficient --- full structural rewrites are required.

- When citing statistics from external sources (e.g. industry reports, platform data), attribute the source explicitly rather than presenting the data as Predis\'s own claim.

- Content must be able to pass standard plagiarism detection tools (Copyscape, Grammarly plagiarism check, etc.) as well as AI content detectors when human-sounding output is required.

- For blog posts and long-form content: each section should reflect independent synthesis of the topic, not a restructured version of an existing article.

**Brand Voice Retention Rules**

All generated content --- regardless of format (blog, ad copy, case study, caption, email) --- must stay true to the Predis.ai voice defined in Section 7. Specific rules:

- **Outcome-first sentences.** Open paragraphs and sections with the result, not the process. ✓ *\"Cut ad creation time by 75% --- without a designer.\"* ✗ *\"Predis.ai uses AI technology to help users create ads more efficiently.\"*

- **Short sentences and punchy phrasing.** Avoid compound sentences that bury the point. If a sentence exceeds 25 words, split it.

- **Second-person address (\"you/your\").** Never slip into third-person when addressing the reader. The content speaks directly to the user.

- **Active voice only.** Passive constructions weaken the brand\'s confident tone. ✓ *\"Predis generates your ad in seconds.\"* ✗ *\"Your ad is generated by Predis in seconds.\"*

- **No filler phrases.** Eliminate: *\"In today\'s fast-paced world\"*, *\"It goes without saying\"*, *\"As we all know\"*, *\"In conclusion\"*, *\"Needless to say\"*.

- **No hollow adjectives.** Avoid: *cutting-edge, revolutionary, robust, seamless, powerful, state-of-the-art* unless backed by a specific claim.

- **Specificity over generality.** Replace vague statements with concrete ones. ✓ *\"Save 90% on photoshoot costs.\"* ✗ *\"Save a lot of money on content production.\"*

- **Preserve the irreverent edge.** Predis copy is occasionally witty and self-assured (*\"Get Envied by the Best Copywriters\"*, *\"Sit back and relax\"*). Agents should match this register --- professional but never corporate-dry.

- **Format consistency by content type:**

  - Blog posts: H2/H3 headers, short intro, numbered or bulleted sections where helpful, one CTA per post

  - Ad copies: ≤3 lines, hook → benefit → CTA structure

  - Captions: conversational, emoji optional, hashtag block at end

  - Case studies: metrics-led, customer voice preserved, structured as Challenge → Solution → Results
